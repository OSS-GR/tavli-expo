Fair play

Turn clocks

  • An online game with a time limit shows a clock for each turn. If your time runs out, you lose that game.
  • A ranked game gives 2 minutes a turn. If your time runs out on two of your turns in a row, also across games of the same match, you lose the match. A turn you play sets the count back to 0.
  • A slow network, a flat battery or a late notification does not undo a result. If our server caused a wrong result, we may correct it.

Ratings and leaderboards

  • A rating is a number that shows how strong you play online. It starts at 1500 and changes after each rated match (the FIBS backgammon formula). Only matches between two saved accounts count, and at most 3 matches of the same two players in one day (UTC). A match that ends because a player deletes their account does not count.
  • Your friends see your rating on the friends board. The global board shows the top players with a username and at least 5 rated matches, to every player with a saved account. You are on it unless you turn off Show me on the global board on the Leaderboards screen.
  • A rating is for fun. It has no money value, it is not a prize and you cannot sell or pass it on.
  • We cannot promise that ratings and boards are always right or always there. We may correct a mistake, count ratings again after a bug, or change how ratings work. If we change how they work for everyone, we tell you in the app first.
