The four Greek games

Same board, same 15 checkers each, four ways to play them.

  • PortesΠόρτες

    The classic game. Hit a lone checker and build your doors.

  • PlakotoΠλακωτό

    No hitting: you pin the other checker under yours. Pin the mother, their last checker on the start point, and they lose 2 points or play on.

  • FevgaΦεύγα

    No hitting and no pinning. One checker holds a point, so you block the way by holding the points in front of the other player.

  • AsodioΑσόδιο

    Roll a 1-2 or a double to open the board, bring in all 15 checkers, then move forward as one army.

Or play a full Tavli match: Portes, Plakoto and Fevga in turn, to 1, 3, 5 or 7 points.

Three ways to play

Against the bot

Easy, Medium or Hard, in every game. The bot plays on your phone, so you need no internet.

Two players, one phone

Take turns on the same phone. The board turns to the player whose turn it is.

A friend online

Send an invite link or a code, and play at your own pace: the app tells you when it is your turn. Add friends and climb the leaderboards. Sign in with Apple or an email code, or start as a guest.

Learn as you play

New to tavli, or to one of the games? The app teaches you at the board.

  • A one-minute lesson on the real board teaches Portes, and every game has a How to play page.
  • Ask for a hint when you are stuck, and look back over a game with the review.
  • Greek and English. Your game saves by itself, so you can continue later.

Privacy

No ads. Games on your phone need no account. You choose if the app may send anonymous usage data, and you can change your answer in Settings. Online play keeps only what it needs to run your games: read the privacy policy.
