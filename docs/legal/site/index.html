Greek tavli for iPhone

Welcome to the kafeneio. Grab a chair.

Tavlaki is Greek tavli for iPhone: Portes, Plakoto, Fevga and Asodio, on a walnut board. Play the computer, a friend on the same phone, or a friend online.

The four Greek games

Same board, same 15 checkers each, four ways to play them.

  • Portes Πόρτες

    The classic game. Hit a lone checker and build your doors.

  • Plakoto Πλακωτό

    No hitting: you pin the other checker under yours. Pin the mother, their last checker on the start point, and you usually win 2 points.

  • Fevga Φεύγα

    No hitting and no pinning. One checker holds a point, so you block the way by holding the points in front of the other player.

  • Asodio Ασόδυο

    Roll a 1-2 or a double to open the board, bring in all 15 checkers, then move forward as one army.

Or a full Tavli match

Portes, Plakoto and Fevga in turn, to 1, 3, 5 or 7 points.

Three ways to play

  • Against the computerEasy, Medium or Hard, in every game. The computer plays on your phone, so you need no internet.
  • Two players, one phoneTake turns on the same phone. The board turns to the player whose turn it is.
  • A friend onlineSend an invite link or a code, and play at your own pace: the app tells you when it is your turn. Add friends and climb the leaderboards. Sign in with Apple or an email code, or start as a guest.

Learn as you play

  • Lessons and How to playShort lessons on the real board, for every game, and a How to play page for each.
  • Hints and game reviewAsk for a hint when you are stuck, and look back over a game with the review.
  • Greek and EnglishYour game saves by itself, so you can continue later.
