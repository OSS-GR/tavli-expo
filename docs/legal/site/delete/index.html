Delete your Tavlaki account

Tavlaki is a Greek backgammon (tavli) app by Orestis Stefanis. You need no account to play on your phone. You have an account only if you played online: a guest account, or an account saved with Sign in with Apple or an email code. This page says how to delete it, what goes and what stays.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Tavlaki and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account. With a guest account, the button is Delete my guest account.
  3. Read what happens, then tap Delete.

The button shows when this phone is signed in to the account. The delete happens at once. For a large account, the server clears the last rows (old invites, blocks, friend requests) in the minutes after.

Deleting the app does not delete your account. Delete the account in the app first, or write to us.

Ask us without the app

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, write to support@tavlaki.com from any email address. Say that you want your account deleted, and tell us how to find it:

  • your username, or your guest name (such as "Guest 4821");
  • with Sign in with Apple or an email code: the email address of that sign-in;
  • for a guest account, also an invite code you made or accepted, your phone model and roughly when you last played: many guests can have the same guest name.

We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete it, for example by writing from the email of your sign-in. We delete the account and answer within one month. If we cannot find the account from what you sent, we tell you.

What is deleted

  • your account and its sign-in sessions; with Sign in with Apple: the Apple id, the email and Apple's tokens; with an email code: your email address;
  • your Sign in with Apple for Tavlaki, if you used it: we ask Apple to cancel it;
  • your guest name and your username (another player may then pick it), and the record of your terms and age answers;
  • your online games in progress: you resign them, so the other player wins;
  • the invites you made;
  • your friendships (also from your friends' lists), the friend requests you sent and got, your friend link, your search times and the friend invites sent to you;
  • your rating and its match counts, so you leave the leaderboards, and your Play ranked request, if you have one;
  • your blocks, the blocks of you by other players, the reports you sent and the reports about you;
  • the chat messages you wrote, your chat mutes, your chat switch and a chat ban, if you have one;
  • your push tokens, so turn notifications stop.

What is kept

  • Finished online games (with their moves) stay for the other player. They hold your old random user id, but no name and no email. Once the account is gone, that id leads to nobody.
  • An invite another player made and you answered stays with that player.
  • The games, the history and the settings on your phone stay on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Crash reports and usage data (only if you allowed them) never hold your account's user id. They age out by themselves: see the privacy policy.

If you play online again after the delete, the app makes a new account with a new user id and a new guest name.
