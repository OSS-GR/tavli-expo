## What Tavlaki is

- **On your phone:** you play against the computer, or with a friend on the same phone, and you can learn with the lessons. This needs no account and no internet.
- **Online:** you play with friends through invites, add friends, play ranked matches with other players, and see ratings and leaderboards. Online play needs an online account (see below).
- Tavlaki is **free**. There are no purchases in the app and no ads.
- Tavlaki is a game of skill and luck for fun. **It is not gambling.** Points, ratings and wins have no money value, and you cannot win prizes. Do not use Tavlaki to bet or to play for money or anything else of value.

## Fair play

When you play online, do not:

- use another program, a backgammon engine, a bot or another person to choose your moves in a **ranked** game. Ranked games give no hints, and nothing else may help you either;
- read or change the dice, or change the app or its messages to the server;
- use a bug to win, or play on after you found one: tell us instead;
- make more than one account to raise a rating, to play yourself, or to get around a block or a step we took against you;
- fix results with another player: lose on purpose, let the clock run out on purpose, or play a friend again and again to move a rating;
- harass anyone through names, invites or friend requests, or send them to annoy;
- send false reports on purpose;
- try to break into, slow down or overload our server, or get data that is not yours.

### Turn clocks

- An online game with a time limit shows a clock for each turn. If your time runs out, you lose that game.
- A ranked game gives **2 minutes** a turn. If your time runs out on **two of your turns in a row**, also across games of the same match, you lose the match. A turn you play sets the count back to 0.
- A slow network, a flat battery or a late notification does not undo a result. If our server caused a wrong result, we may correct it.

## Reports and blocks

- To report a player, tap **Report or block** where their name shows: in the menu of an online game, at the end of an online match, in History or in Friends. Pick one reason: an offensive name, cheating or abuse of the game, harassment, or something else. There is no text box. A report also blocks the player.
- A player you block cannot send you new invites or play ranked with you, and their name no longer shows to you. A friendship between you ends. A game you play with them now goes on. To unblock them, go to **Settings → Blocked players**.
- The other player is not told about a block or a report.
- We read every report. A report alone does not punish anyone: we look at what happened first.
- You can also write to us, also if you have no account. Tell us the player's name, where you saw it (for example the invite code of a game) and why you think it breaks these terms or the law.

## Ratings and leaderboards

- A rating is a number that shows how strong you play online. It starts at 1500 and changes after each rated match (the FIBS backgammon formula). Only matches between two saved accounts count, and at most 3 matches of the same two players in one day (UTC). A match that ends because a player deletes their account does not count.
- Your friends see your rating on the friends board. The global board shows the top players with a username and at least 5 rated matches, to every player with a saved account. You are on it unless you turn off **Show me on the global board** on the Leaderboards screen.
- A rating is for fun. It has no money value, it is not a prize and you cannot sell or pass it on.
- We cannot promise that ratings and boards are always right or always there. We may correct a mistake, count ratings again after a bug, or change how ratings work. If we change how they work for everyone, we tell you in the app first.
