## When these terms apply

- You can play on your phone, against the bot or a friend next to you, without accepting anything here.
- These terms apply when you **play online**. You accept them when you pick a username. By playing online as a guest, you agree to follow them too.

## Age

To pick a username, you must be **at least 16**. In countries outside the EU and the EEA, and in the EU and EEA countries that set 13, the age is **13**. The app asks the age that fits your phone's region.

If you are younger, please do not pick a username. You can still play on your phone. <!-- OWNER: a lawyer checks the age rule and the country list (apps/mobile/src/features/online/usernameStep.ts). -->

## Your online account

- **Guest account:** the app makes it when you first play online. It has a random guest name, such as "Guest 4821". Only that phone can open it.
- **Saved account:** with Sign in with Apple you can open the same account on a new phone, and pick a username.
- Keep your phone and your Apple account safe. What happens in your online games is on your account.
- You can delete your online data at any time: **Settings → Privacy → Delete my online data**.

## Usernames

A username is what other players see in games, invites and lists. You pick it, so it is your content.

- It has 3 to 16 characters: Latin or Greek letters (not both), digits and `_`. It starts with a letter.
- It is unique. Names that differ only in capitals, accents or letters that look alike (such as `I` and `l`) count as the same name.
- You can change it once in 30 days. The first change after your first pick can happen at once.
- A username must **not**:
  - pretend to be another person, a famous person or Tavlaki (for example "admin" or "support");
  - insult, threaten or harass anyone, or attack a group of people;
  - be sexual, violent or about drugs;
  - hold someone's personal data, such as a phone number or an address;
  - advertise anything, or hold a link or a web address.
- The app blocks some words by itself. That list cannot catch everything, so the rules above still apply.
- If a username breaks these rules, we may change it to a guest name or remove it. If it happens again, we may stop the account from playing online.

To report a username, write to us with the name and, if you can, the invite code of a game with that player. <!-- OWNER: the server can keep reports since #320; the in-app Report and Block buttons are #435. Then say here how to report in the app. -->

## Fair play

When you play online, do not:

- use another program to play your moves for you, or to read the dice before they are rolled;
- use a bug to win, or play on after you found one: tell us instead;
- try to break into, slow down or overload our server, or get data that is not yours;
- make many accounts to annoy other players or to get around a block.

We may end a game, or stop an account from playing online, if it breaks these rules.
