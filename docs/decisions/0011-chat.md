# ADR-0011: Chat in online games

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-11-25: free text, built in full)
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Issue:** #693 (this ADR), epic #692

## Context

Today two players in an online game can only play moves. ADR-0009 item 7
says "No chat", and the terms, the store listing and the age rating say
the same. Open question Q16 (2026-11-02) said "Chat: free text, later".

The owner now wants a chat in online games, in friend games and in Play
ranked (2026-11-25), and picked **free text** over ready phrases.

Free text is content that players write to each other, also to strangers
in Play ranked. That brings duties we do not have today:

- App Store rule 1.2 and Google's user content policy ask for a filter, a
  way to report a message, a way to block the sender, and action by the
  owner on a report within 24 hours.
- The age rating goes up in both stores ("Messaging and chat").
- Messages are personal data: the privacy policy, the account delete,
  Download my data and the store privacy answers must cover them.
- One person (the owner) reads the reports. The design must keep that
  work small.

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost |
|--------|------|------|------|
| A. Ready phrases and emoji only | No bad words are possible; no moderation; each phone shows its own language; the age rating stays | Not what the owner wants; players cannot agree on anything ("one more?") | Size M (about 4 PRs) |
| B. Free text for every online player, guests too | Most open | A guest accepted no terms and passed no age check; a banned guest makes a new guest in one tap; hardest to moderate | Size L |
| C. Free text for saved accounts with a username, with limits, a filter, mute, block and report | Every chatter accepted the terms and the age check and has a name the owner can act on; a ban costs the player a real account | Guests cannot chat; about 8 PRs; owner work after release | Size L (about 8 PRs) |

## Decision

We choose **C**. It replaces item 7 of ADR-0009 ("No chat"); the rest of
ADR-0009 holds, also "never a fake player".

### Who may chat

1. Chat exists only in online games between two people: friend games and
   Play ranked. Never vs the bot, pass-and-play or the tutorial.
2. A player may send and read only with a **saved account** (ADR-0008)
   that has a **username** and accepted the current terms (which get chat
   rules; `TERMS_VERSION` goes up). So both the age check and the terms
   stand behind every message.
3. The chat of a game is open only when **both** players may chat. If one
   is a guest, neither sees a chat: no half chat.
4. Only the two players of a game can read or write its chat. There is no
   watcher role.

### What a message is

5. Text only: no pictures, no voice, no files.
6. At most `CHAT_MAX_LENGTH` = 200 characters after a trim. Line breaks
   become spaces. An empty message is refused.
7. No links: the server refuses a message with a web address or an email
   address (`LINK`). This stops spam and most ways to take a child to
   another app.
8. At most `CHAT_MESSAGES_PER_MINUTE` = 10 from one player in one game.
   This stops a flood. There is no cap per game (owner, 2026-11-25): two
   players may talk as long as they like.

### Safety

9. **Filter.** Ordinary swearing is allowed (owner, 2026-11-25): players
   at a tavli board swear, and adults may talk as they like. The server
   refuses only a short list of **slurs** (words that attack a person for
   their race, origin or sexuality) and words about the abuse of
   children (`BAD_WORDS`), with the same letter folding as usernames
   (case, accents, Greek and Latin look-alikes, Greeklish). The stores
   ask for a filter for chat with strangers (Apple 1.2), so the list is
   not empty. It refuses; it does not replace letters with stars. The
   sender sees why. Mute, block and report handle the rest. The chat
   list is its own list: the username list stays stricter.
10. **Mute.** One tap hides the chat of this game for you. The other
    player is not told. A new message then shows nothing and makes no
    sound.
11. **The Settings switch.** "Chat in online games": **On** (the
    default) or **Off**. Off acts as a mute of every game. The server
    keeps it, so it holds on every phone of the account.
12. **Block.** A block (#320) also closes the chat between the two at
    once, both ways: the blocker sees no old or new message of the
    other, and the blocked player's send is taken with no error and
    shown to nobody (they are never told, as with invites, #439).
13. **Report.** Report gets the reason `chat`. A report with that reason
    keeps a copy of the reported player's last `REPORT_CHAT_MESSAGES` =
    20 messages of that game with the report, so the owner can judge
    after the chat is deleted. This is the first free text in `reports`.
    A report still blocks too.
14. **The owner's tools** (internal functions, never public): read a
    report with its messages, delete a player's messages, and stop a
    player's chat (a ban: for a number of days, or for good). A banned
    player sees the status `banned` and cannot send; the other player
    sees only "no chat". The owner acts on a report within 24 hours
    (Apple 1.2).
15. Ranked and friend games have the same rules. There is no softer set
    for friends: a friend link adds a friend in one tap (#638).

### Data

16. Messages live in their own table, one row per message, with the
    game, the sender, the text and the time. They are not part of the
    stored game or the match record, and the game review never shows
    them.
17. Messages are deleted `CHAT_KEEP_DAYS` = 30 days after the match
    ends (a scheduled job, in pages). A copy inside a report stays until
    the owner deletes the report.
18. The account delete deletes the caller's messages (in pages), their
    mutes and their setting. Download my data holds the caller's own
    messages, never the other player's.
19. The text of a message never goes to analytics, crash reports, pushes
    or logs. Analytics may count that a message was sent (no text, no
    ids).
20. No push for a chat message in the first version. A push would carry
    text from a stranger to a lock screen.

### In the app

21. A chat button on the online game screen, with a dot for unread
    messages. It opens a sheet over the board with the messages and one
    text field. The board is not covered by default.
22. The first time a player opens a chat, a short step shows the chat
    rules and where to mute, block and report.
23. A screen reader reads a new message through `useAnnouncement`, and
    the sheet works with the move list.

### How it is built

- The port is `Chat` (`packages/contracts/src/chat.ts`): `watch(gameId,
  onChat, onError)`, `send({ gameId, text })`, `setMuted(gameId, muted)`,
  `settings()` and `setEnabled(enabled)`. Contract checks: `chatContract()`
  in `@tavli/contracts/testing`.
- The pure rules are in `@tavli/online` (`chat.ts`): `planChatMessage`
  (the trim, the length, the link check, the filter, the limits, the
  ban, the block) and `chatSeenBy` (what one player sees). Both servers
  run them; the Convex functions never copy a check.
- The in-memory server gets `chatFor(playerId)`. The Convex side is
  `convex/chat.ts` with the tables `chatMessages`, `chatMutes` and
  `chatBans`, and the setting on the `playerNames` row. The adapter is
  `createConvexChat` (`CHAT_FUNCTIONS` in `@tavli/backend-convex`).
- A send is one mutation that reads the caller with `existingPlayer`.
  A refused message returns its reason as a value where a count must
  survive (as `requestByUsername` does).

## Consequences

- Easier: players can talk ("one more?", "good game"). Play ranked feels
  less like a machine.
- Harder: the owner must read chat reports every day. The age rating
  rises (Apple likely 12+ or more; the form decides). The terms get chat
  rules and every saved player accepts them again before the first
  message. The privacy policy, the store privacy answers and the delete
  pages change.
- Harder: every new table here (`chatMessages`, `chatMutes`, `chatBans`)
  joins the account delete, Download my data and the guest move rules of
  CLAUDE.md. A guest has no messages, so the guest move has nothing to
  move, but it must not break.
- Risk: the chat allows swearing, so a player may meet rude words from a
  stranger in Play ranked. Mute, block, report and the ban are the tools,
  and the age rating answers must say so ("Profanity or crude humor").
- Not built now: pictures, voice, emoji reactions, a chat outside a
  game, chat pushes, a watcher's chat, an automatic translation.
- Switching later: the `Chat` port hides the server. A vendor filter or
  a moderation service can sit behind `planChatMessage` with no change
  to the app. Ready phrases (option A) can be added on top as buttons
  that send a fixed text.
