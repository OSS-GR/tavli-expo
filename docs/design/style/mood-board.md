## What we learned

- **The fold is the bar.** Greek boards are a folding box. The two halves meet in the middle. Hinges sit on the fold. We draw the bar as two frame rails with a seam and (in B) brass hinges.
- **Bear-off trays are not standard** on Greek boards. Boards "with slots" have them at the ends. We use one slot at the home end, split in two (one half per player).
- **Points facing each other use different woods.** Every real board alternates diagonally.
- **Checkers:** turned wood with grooves (olive, walnut) or resin with a dished centre (ivory, black, red).
- **Dice:** small, white or ivory, black pips. Some sets have a red ace pip.
- **Keep the field calm.** On every good board the field is one quiet wood; the decoration sits in the frame and the points.
